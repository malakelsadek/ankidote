Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

ankidote is a study app for medical students. You can make question decks by hand or import them, and you can upload lecture slides and have an AI write exam-style questions from them. This page explains what information the app collects, what it is used for, who else handles it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

ankidote is run by [YOUR FULL NAME], based in Egypt. You can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign in with an emailed link, we receive your email address.
  • Your study data. Your decks, questions, folders, review history, progress statistics and settings (such as theme, sound and zoom).
  • Slides you upload. The PDF file, the text of each page, and a cover image of the first page.
  • Questions generated for you, including the explanations and which slides they came from.
  • Usage and plan information. How many AI generation runs and questions you have used, and your plan status and renewal date.
  • On your device. The app stores some settings and a copy of your session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and the app remembers your preferences.

We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers in the app.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your decks and progress in your account, so they are available on all your devices.
  • To generate questions from the slides you choose.
  • To enforce free-trial and plan limits and show you your usage.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information.

Your uploaded slides

Slides are stored in private storage and are only accessible from your account. Please upload only material you have the right to use. You are responsible for having permission to upload your slides.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your account details, decks, review history and uploaded slides. Copies in routine backups may remain for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time in Settings.
  • You can export your decks and progress from the Data page.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • You can clear the app's local storage in your browser at any time. You will be signed out.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each user's data is separated by account-level access rules in the database. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

ankidote is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Study content is not medical advice

Questions written by the AI can be wrong. Always check them against your course material. ankidote is a study aid, not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add payments. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about any significant change.
